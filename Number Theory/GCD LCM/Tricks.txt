04. Primitive Pythagorean triples are sets of three positive integers (a,b,c) that 
        i. satisfy the Pythagorean theorem: a² + b² = c²
        ii. are coprime (the greatest common divisor of a, b, c is 1)
                
                How to Generate Primitive Pythagorean Triples
        Generate all primitive Pythagorean triples using two positive integers m and n where:
        i. m > n
        ii. m and n are coprime [GCD(m,n) = 1]
        iii. m − n is odd (one is even and one is odd).
        iv. Then the triple is given by:
                                        a = m² - n²
                                        b = 2mn
                                        c = m² + n²
